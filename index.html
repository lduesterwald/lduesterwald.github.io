About Me

I'm a senior studying Computer Science at Cornell University. My research interests center on understanding how language models learn from their training data, particularly how data properties, prior learning, and training objectives and architectures influence the acquisition and memorization of information.

Currently, I work with Professor Chris De Sa and Professor A. Feder Cooper on memorization in language models. Our work investigates how information learned during training can be extracted from language models, and I'm currently studying memorization in diffusion language models.

Previously, I conducted research at Carnegie Mellon University with Professor Norman Sadeh on LLM-based cybersecurity assistants and personalization, and at Microsoft Research with Dr. Suman Nath on AI agents and automated refinement of vibe-coded applications. I've also worked on numerical claim verification at Cornell and machine learning for genomic analysis at Argonne National Laboratory.
